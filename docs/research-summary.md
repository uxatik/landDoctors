# LandDoctor — Research summary

Copy of the project doc `claude/landdoctor-research-summary.md`. Full report: https://claude.ai/artifact/Td85f6i6Xhp23KYSAa4nTJ

## Users
- Rural and peri-urban landowners, buyers, heirs; many older, phone-first, on cheap Android phones with costly data.
- Expatriates (NRB) whose families own land; comfortable with links and cards.
- They distrust online payment to strangers and fear middlemen. Plain Bangla and published prices build trust.

## Market facts used in copy
- About 8 million people a year face land disputes; about a quarter are resolved.
- About half of households using land services report corruption (TIB 2023).
- Government e-mutation fee total about ৳1,170; fees must be paid online; land hotline 16122.

## Product rules from research
- Sell clarity, correct paperwork and field work. Never "faster approval".
- Free short triage call before payment; payment by bKash/Nagad via gateway.
- Hotline and WhatsApp alongside the website.
- Show official government fees separately.
- Don't store NID or deeds in phase 1.

## Key risks relevant to the build
- Data leaks of personal data from a vibe-coded app.
- Payments trusted from the browser.
- Consultants who are government employees without sanction.
- Customers taking consultants offline (no consultant phone numbers on public pages).
